Welcome to the Career Guide

The Assam Forest Department works to protect forests, wildlife and natural resources. It offers different career opportunities for candidates with different educational qualifications.

Forest Department Jobs

Forest Guard

Protects forests, monitors illegal activities and helps conserve wildlife.

Qualification: Check the latest recruitment notification.

Recruitment: Written test and physical tests, as notified.

Forester Grade-I

Supervises forest protection activities and assists in forest management.

Qualification: Check the latest recruitment notification.

Recruitment: Selection process as notified.

Forest Ranger

Manages forest ranges, staff and conservation activities.

Qualification: Relevant bachelor's degree as prescribed.

Recruitment: Competitive examination and other notified stages.

Wildlife Biologist

Studies animals, habitats and wildlife conservation.

Qualification: Relevant biology or wildlife-related degree.

Recruitment: Depends on the recruiting organisation.

Forest Surveyor

Helps with forest mapping, land surveys and field measurements.

Qualification: Depends on the specific post.

Recruitment: As specified in the vacancy notification.

Forest Officer

Works in forest administration, conservation and management.

Qualification: Depends on the officer-level post.

Recruitment: Through the relevant state or national recruitment process.

Which Subjects Should You Study?

  • General Knowledge
  • General English
  • Mathematics
  • Reasoning
  • General Science
  • Environment and Ecology
  • Current Affairs

Always follow the syllabus of the specific recruitment notification.

Which Degree Can Help?

Depending on the post, relevant degrees may include:

  • B.Sc. Forestry
  • B.Sc. Botany
  • B.Sc. Zoology
  • B.Sc. Agriculture
  • Environmental Science
  • Other eligible science or engineering degrees where specified

Career Opportunities Outside Assam

You can also explore forest and wildlife careers in other states and at the national level.

  • Indian Forest Service (IFoS)
  • State Forest Departments
  • Wildlife Research Organisations
  • Environmental and Conservation Organisations

Salary and Difficulty

Salary depends on the post, pay level, allowances and current government rules. Check the official notification for accurate starting pay.

Difficulty also varies. Some posts require physical fitness tests, while officer-level examinations can require extensive preparation.
